# 2-Minute Demo Script: Law AI Tutor - Commercial

**Before recording**
- Use a **Chrome Incognito** window (proves the live URL works for anyone, with no saved data).
- Open https://law-ai-tutor-commercial.vercel.app. Close other tabs and turn notifications off.
- Record with **Windows + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) or Loom (loom.com, free). Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** or keep the Loom link, and check that it opens in Incognito.
- Have this answer ready to paste (it is deliberately weak):
  `Answer: The insurer must pay Carlo. Legal basis: Carlo paid the premium. Application: The policy was still in force when the car burned. Conclusion: Carlo can collect.`
